Shape Shift

Match a color or a shape, make your next choice, and keep the run going. Play directly in your browser with mouse, touch or keyboard.

Play Shape Shift

One matching rule, different challenges

Your current icon shows a color and a shape. Choose a board tile that shares either property before the timer runs out. Each correct choice changes your icon, so keep watching both the board and your next target.

  • Endless: chase your personal best as the pace increases. Collect useful pickups and use boosts to protect your run.
  • Adventure: explore 100 levels with different board challenges and optional star tasks.
  • Shape Drop: aim falling pieces and bring matching shapes together.
  • Shape Slice: swipe through flying shapes while avoiding bombs.
  • Piano Flow: tap four lanes to play repeating musical phrases, and sustain the longer notes.

Keyboard controls

In Adventure and Endless, use WASD to select a tile and Space to pick it. Press 1, 2 or 3 for boosts and Z to pause or go back. Use W/S to navigate menus and Space or Enter to confirm.

In Shape Drop, hold A/D to aim and press S to drop. In Piano Flow, use A/S/D/F for the four lanes. Shape Slice uses mouse dragging or touch swipes.

Getting started

New players begin with an Endless tutorial. Coins, experience, quests and achievements unlock more ways to play. Your progress is saved in your browser; use the same browser and device to continue your local progress. Music and sound effects can be adjusted separately in Settings.

The game includes its privacy information in Settings. This homepage does not load advertising or analytics scripts.
