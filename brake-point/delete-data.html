Brake Point: delete your online data

MK Playcraft Studio offers an optional leaderboard in Brake Point Android version 0.114 and later.

Delete from the game

  1. Open Brake Point and enter Garage.
  2. Tap your player nickname.
  3. Tap Remove online entry.

This deletes your online player profile, nickname, best distance and run tickets from the leaderboard service. It keeps your local diamonds, rides, upgrades and saved progress. Joining again creates a new profile.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email mk.studio.customersupport@gmail.com with the subject Brake Point data deletion. Provide your nickname and, if you have it, player ID. We may need information to verify which entry belongs to you. Never send passwords or your private access token.

Retention and local data

Online profiles remain until deleted. Temporary run tickets are removed within approximately 25 hours. Routine service-provider security records and support correspondence may have separate retention periods. Uninstalling the app does not delete its online profile. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes local game progress and can permanently erase it.

These controls apply to the developer's leaderboard records. For advertising-provider data, see the Brake Point privacy policy.
