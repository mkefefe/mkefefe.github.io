Brake Point Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

MK Playcraft Studio publishes Brake Point. This policy covers the offline Android game, package com.mkplaycraft.brakepoint, distributed through Google Play. Browser versions hosted by other platforms may have different services and privacy practices.

Information used on your device

The game saves progress, best distances, earned diamonds, unlocked rides, upgrades, and preferences in its private on-device storage. These records are used to continue your progress and remember settings. The game does not send these records to MK Playcraft Studio or operate an online leaderboard or cloud-save service.

No account, tracking or advertising in this Android release

You do not need an account. This release does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifier. The game does not request Android internet permission. Any unavailable video-reward controls do not load ads or transmit data. There are no real-money purchases in this release; diamonds are earned through gameplay.

Support messages

If you contact mk.studio.customersupport@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Email is processed by our email service provider, Google. We do not sell your support information or use it for advertising.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear Brake Point's app storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently erase progress. The developer cannot restore local progress from a server. There is no game account to delete. For support correspondence, contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion. We keep correspondence only as needed to resolve support requests and meet applicable legal obligations; email-provider backups and security records may have their own retention periods.

Security and service providers

Game saves are held in Android's app-private storage. No security measure is perfect. Google Play and Android may process installation, diagnostic or security information under their own settings and privacy policies; this is separate from the game's local saves. The public copy of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website may involve routine connection information such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy policy. The policy page does not add analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Children's privacy

The Android game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child has sent personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the Android game's data practices change and provide additional notice or consent where required. For privacy questions or requests, contact MK Playcraft Studio at mk.studio.customersupport@gmail.com.
