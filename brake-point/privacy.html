Brake Point Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026; online leaderboard applies from Android version 0.114

MK Playcraft Studio publishes Brake Point. This policy covers the Android game, package com.mkplaycraft.brakepoint, distributed through Google Play. Browser versions hosted by other platforms may have different services and privacy practices.

Information used on your device

The game saves progress, best distances, earned diamonds, unlocked rides, upgrades, and preferences in its private on-device storage. These records are used to continue your progress and remember settings. Diamonds, rides, upgrades and settings remain local; there is no cloud-save service. If you choose to join the online leaderboard, your nickname and best distance are sent as described below.

Optional online leaderboard

Starting with version 0.114, you can view the online leaderboard and optionally join by saving a nickname in Garage. Use a generated nickname rather than your real name. The service stores a random player ID, a hashed access token, your chosen public nickname, best distance, and temporary run tickets to validate submissions. Your nickname and best distance appear publicly on the leaderboard. No email address or password is required. A private access token stays in app storage to authorize profile changes and score submissions. Cloudflare hosts the service and processes network metadata, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect it. Connections use HTTPS. These records are used for the leaderboard, profile management and abuse prevention, not advertising.

Online participation is optional and does not affect local progress. In Garage, open your player name and choose Remove online entry to delete the server profile and its run tickets. Your local progress is preserved. Profiles remain until removed; run tickets are cleared within approximately 25 hours. Removing or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete the online entry. You can also contact support to request deletion, providing your nickname and player ID if available; never send your private access token.

Earlier versions

Android versions before 0.111 contain no advertising SDK. Android versions before 0.114 do not connect to the online leaderboard.

Optional rewarded advertising

Version 0.111 adds Google AdMob rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch an ad to continue a run or earn 50 diamonds. Declining an ad does not prevent normal play. A reward is granted only after the ad provider confirms completion. No real-money purchases are offered.

Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform may process your IP address (including approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or app identifiers to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. This data is transmitted to Google over encrypted connections. We disable the Android advertising ID permission and apply child-directed, under-age-of-consent treatment and a G ad-content limit for all players. No personalized advertising is requested. These settings do not mean that the ad SDK collects no data.

We do not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access. Ad privacy choices are available in Settings where required. For Google's practices and data controls, see Google's Privacy Policy. Diamonds and upgrades stay on your device; only the optional leaderboard information described above is sent to our leaderboard service.

Support messages

If you contact mk.studio.customersupport@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Email is processed by our email service provider, Google. We do not sell your support information or use it for advertising.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear Brake Point's app storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently erase progress. The developer cannot restore local progress from a server. The optional leaderboard profile can be removed separately using the in-game control described above. For support correspondence, contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion. We keep correspondence only as needed to resolve support requests and meet applicable legal obligations; email-provider backups and security records may have their own retention periods.

Security and service providers

Game saves are held in Android's app-private storage. No security measure is perfect. Google Play and Android may process installation, diagnostic or security information under their own settings and privacy policies; this is separate from the game's local saves. The public copy of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website may involve routine connection information such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy policy. The policy page does not add analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Children's privacy

The game is available to children. Advertising requests use child-protective settings for every player, and the game does not request a real name or email address for gameplay. The optional leaderboard uses a public nickname; players should use generated names and never enter personal information. Google may process technical data as described above to provide ads and protect against fraud. If a child has sent personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the Android game's data practices change and provide additional notice or consent where required. For privacy questions or requests, contact MK Playcraft Studio at mk.studio.customersupport@gmail.com.
