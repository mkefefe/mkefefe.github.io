Brake Point

An arcade game for Android. Keep your run going, build your best distance, and earn diamonds to unlock rides and upgrades.

Closed testing

Brake Point is currently offered through a closed test. Access requires an invited Google account and an eligible Play Store region. A newly submitted update becomes available after Google completes its review.

Support

For help with the game, contact mk.studio.customersupport@gmail.com. Include your game version, device model, and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords.

Privacy

Progress is saved on your device. Versions with advertising offer optional rewarded ads to continue a run or earn diamonds. Read the Brake Point privacy policy for details.
